A Boutique Hotel
Redefining Kitale

Located on Moi Avenue in the heart of Kitale town, Cranes Suites is Trans Nzoia County's finest boutique hotel. Housed in a beautifully refurbished high-rise, we offer 18 uniquely styled rooms combining modern elegance with warm Kenyan hospitality.

From our celebrated rooftop restaurant with its panoramic views of Mt. Elgon, to our state-of-the-art conference facilities, every detail has been crafted to elevate your stay.

Signature Experience
Rooftop Experience

Dine Above
Kitale

Our rooftop restaurant and bar is one of Kitale's most celebrated culinary destinations. Perched above the city skyline with unobstructed views of Mt. Elgon, it's the perfect setting for a romantic sundowner or a business dinner.

Sip on a vintage wine or enjoy our famous Sirwo Coffee as the sun dips behind Mt. Elgon. Our kitchen specialises in both local Kenyan favourites and international cuisine, crafted fresh from the trans-nzoia region's abundant produce.
